PRIVACY · DATENSCHUTZ

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

1. Responsible developer

CASH – Money Counter is independently developed and provided by Tim Fritzsche, Augustastr. 7, 06108 Halle, Germany. Contact: tim@wellithink.org.

2. No tracking or advertising

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technology or user accounts. The developer does not collect usage profiles and does not sell personal data.

3. Data stored locally

Currency selections, counting entries, saved counts, optional names, dates, language preferences and app settings are stored locally on the user’s device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or synchronized with a cloud service. Users can remove saved entries in the app; uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data.

4. Language and regional settings

The app may read the device language and regional format locally to suggest a language or currency. CASH does not request GPS access and does not collect or transmit location data.

5. Voluntary feedback by email

The feedback function opens the user’s configured email application. Nothing is sent until the user reviews the message and actively sends it. The sender address, message content and any technical details visible in the draft are then processed by the relevant email providers and received by the developer solely to answer the request and improve the app. Users can edit or remove the prepared content before sending and may request deletion of their correspondence by email.

6. Optional in-app tips

CASH offers an optional, one-time in-app tip to support development. Tips are processed entirely through Apple's or Google's in-app purchase systems; the app and its developer never receive, see or store any payment, billing or card details. Purchases, billing history and refund requests are handled directly by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. Tipping is entirely optional and does not unlock any feature of the app.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Questions about privacy can be sent to tim@wellithink.org.

Die App enthält keine Werbung, Analyse- oder Trackingtechnologien und keine Benutzerkonten. Der Entwickler erstellt keine Nutzungsprofile und verkauft keine personenbezogenen Daten.

4. Sprach- und Regionseinstellungen

Die App kann die auf dem Gerät eingestellte Sprache und das Regionsformat lokal auslesen, um eine Sprache oder Währung vorzuschlagen. CASH fordert keinen GPS-Zugriff an und erfasst oder übermittelt keine Standortdaten.
